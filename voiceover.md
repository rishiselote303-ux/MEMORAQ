# MEMORAQ — Video Voiceover Script

**Runtime target:** ~2:30–2:50 at a natural pace (~150 wpm)
**Tone:** Confident, editorial, a little wry. Pause after each numbered block.

---

**[0:00 — Hook]**

Every company has a scar-tissue problem. Decisions get made, the results land in a spreadsheet, and the reasoning evaporates. A year later, the same debate restarts from zero — with no record of what was tried, what it cost, or what it taught.

**[0:22 — The premise]**

This is MEMORAQ — a strategy co-pilot built on one stubborn principle: the LLM is never allowed to do the math.

**[0:32 — What it is]**

MEMORAQ is a memory-augmented strategic intelligence platform. It pairs a persistent organizational memory with deterministic financial and simulation engines — so every recommendation is grounded in real retained experience, and every number comes from code, not from a language model.

**[0:55 — The memory]**

The memory spine is Hindsight — an open-source, self-hosted memory engine. Every decision, outcome, and competitor move is retained into dedicated banks. On demand, the system recalls the relevant past and reflects over it, with the sources cited right on screen. You can watch it happen live in the Memory Inspector: every row is a real operation, not a mock-up.

**[1:22 — The engines]**

The intelligence comes from somewhere else entirely. A deterministic six-month financial engine prices the present. A what-if simulator rehearses the future. Drag the price lever, and you instantly see projected revenue, margin, and profit deltas — plus the historical experiments most similar to the one you're about to run.

**[1:47 — The demo]**

In the demo workspace, you're advising Northwind Instruments. Ask the executive AI a question, and it recalls from memory, computes with code, and answers with the evidence attached — assumptions labeled as assumptions, hypotheses labeled as hypotheses.

**[2:07 — The loop]**

Then comes the part most tools skip. When the decision is made, one click retains it — decision, rationale, and eventual outcome — back into memory. The next decision starts from experience. The intelligence compounds.

**[2:25 — Trust]**

And if the memory server goes offline? Nothing pretends. The app switches to a clearly labeled fallback mode. No silent guessing. No fabricated confidence.

**[2:40 — Close]**

MEMORAQ. Remember the past. Understand the present. Simulate the future — and leave the judgment where it belongs: with humans.

Self-hosted memory. Deterministic math. The co-pilot serious decisions deserve.

*[Music out. End card: MEMORAQ — institutional memory for serious decisions.]*
